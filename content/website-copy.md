## ABOUT US PAGE

### Heading

Pacific Industrial Sourcing — Connecting the World's Best Manufacturers to New Zealand and

### Who We Are

Pacific Industrial Sourcing was founded in Auckland, New Zealand, with one clear purpose — to
make international trade simpler, more transparent, and more accessible for industrial
businesses on both sides of the Pacific.

With over 17 years of hands-on experience in import, export, machinery, and industrial
procurement, we bring deep market knowledge, verified supplier networks, and genuine trade
expertise to every partnership we build.

We are not a marketplace. We are not a listing service. We are your dedicated sourcing and
procurement partner — working alongside you like a trusted colleague, not just a service
provider.

### What We Do

We started as a sourcing and procurement agency helping professional buyers navigate the
complexities of global trade. Over time, we expanded our capabilities by combining human
intelligence, long-standing supplier relationships, and local compliance expertise to deliver a
truly end-to-end service.

Today, our services span across industrial products, construction materials, heavy engineering
components, and healthcare equipment — all sourced and verified to meet AS/NZS compliance
standards, in partnership with accredited testing laboratories.

We also work the other way. We help New Zealand and Australian businesses go global — finding
international buyers and connecting domestic suppliers to markets across Asia and beyond.

### How We Work

For every buyer, three things determine success in global trade:

- **Uncompromising Quality** — Products must meet exact specifications, every time, without compromise
- **Competitive Pricing** — Costs aligned with market expectations while protecting your margins
- **Reliable Delivery** — On-time, every time — because your supply chain depends on it

We back these with:

- **Strategic Sourcing** — Precise matching of verified manufacturers to your exact product needs
- **Supplier Vetting** — Rigorous checks for integrity, capacity, financial standing, and compliance — no traders, no shortcuts
- **End-to-End Support** — From first inquiry through negotiation, documentation, customs, and delivery

Our goal is simple: free you from the complexity of global sourcing so you can focus on what
matters most — growing your business and serving your customers.

### Our Promise

Trust and confidentiality are not features we offer — they are the foundation of everything we
do. Every supplier relationship, every commercial term, and every client interest is handled
with absolute discretion and professionalism.

We are proud of the partnerships we have built across the Pacific. Many of our clients have been
with us since the very beginning — and we look forward to building many more years of shared
success together.

### Why Pacific Industrial Sourcing

- **17+ years** — Import, export and industrial procurement experience
- **Verified manufacturers only** — No traders, no marketplaces, no shortcuts
- **AS/NZS compliant** — Accredited testing lab partnerships for full compliance confidence
- **Two-way trade** — Asia to NZ/AU and NZ/AU to global markets
- **End-to-end** — Sourcing, compliance, logistics, customs — all under one roof
- **SME focused** — Built specifically for New Zealand's industrial small and medium businesses
